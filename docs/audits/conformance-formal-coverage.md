## Effect of screen-local navigation

The earlier audit attributed 196 failures to layouts with a tiny shortcut on `chartSummary` and a usable detour on `directory`. Both screens called their link `toList`. Lean scopes IDs by screen. The driver now excludes only the failed screen/control pair and traverses `toDirectory` followed by `toList` for all five v2 personas. Those historical failures can no longer serve as counterexamples. [Driver and regression test](../../conformance/driver.test.mjs)

Those fields are wristband scan, medication scan, high-alert cosign, consent verification, pain reassessment, naloxone offer and naloxone education. The macro probes additionally have to reveal the specific injected control beside the first matching field. A hidden duplicate could prevent that before the entry trace check. ## Remaining work

Every scenario now has a whole-trace guarantee. Checker acceptance implies that each required commit has an actionable refusal for every required entry, confirmed human entry and a successful signature ([`accepted_actionableEntries`](../../lean/Ehr/Check/EntryFamilySound.lean)). It also implies that every clinical encounter in the catalog has a native witness through its reviews, receipts, alerts, probes and signature ([`accepted_clinicalScenarios`](../../lean/Ehr/Check/ClinicalScenarioSound.lean)).

These guarantees are existential and fixture-specific: a conforming native execution exists for the specified encounter and fixture patient, which is the same scope as the test. Three obligations remain open beyond the suite:

1. **Completion from arbitrary reachable states.** The proofs witness completion from chart opening, not from every state a clinician can reach. The existing feasibility obstruction shows that this needs more than structural rules. [Feasibility obstruction](../../lean/Ehr/Check/FeasibleV1Obstruction.lean)
2. **Other patients and data.** Each witness uses its scenario's fixture. A different patient, allergy list or result set can take a different route.
3. **Conservative search.** The bounded witness search can reject a safe layout when it finds no witness within its bounds. It never accepts one without a witness.

The existing feasibility obstruction demonstrates why structural completability does not establish positive completion for an arbitrary accepted layout. [Workflow proofs](../../lean/Ehr/Check/WorkflowSound.lean) · [Completion witness](../../lean/Ehr/Check/CompletionScenario.lean) · [Feasibility obstruction](../../lean/Ehr/Check/FeasibleV1Obstruction.lean)
