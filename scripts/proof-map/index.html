How to read the map

Click a node to expand or collapse its direct neighbors. “Read” opens its statement; “Source” opens the built commit on GitHub. Hover or focus a name to preview its full type and docstring. Enter in search jumps to the first result; ↓ moves into the results.

Proof citations include definitions used in the body. Statement references are shown separately. Repeated declarations can appear on different paths; cycles stop at the path boundary.
